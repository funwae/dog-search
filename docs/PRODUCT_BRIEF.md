# Product Brief

## Product name
LostDog Deep Search MCP

## Primary user
An owner or helper trying to locate a missing dog quickly.

## Success definition
The system finds high-quality leads faster than manual searching by:
- centralizing evidence,
- reducing duplicate review work,
- surfacing the most geographically and visually plausible candidates,
- preserving a shareable case log.

## Inputs
- 1 to 10 dog images
- flyer text or structured intake
- last seen location and date/time
- sex, breed guess, collar status, chip status, medical urgency
- optional owner-authorized browsing session

## Outputs
- ranked lead list
- annotated confidence reasons
- screenshot/evidence archive
- exportable case brief

## Non-goals
- private-access bypass
- autonomous outreach
- facial recognition of people
- general-purpose stalking/search product
